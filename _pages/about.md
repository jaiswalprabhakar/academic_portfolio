I'm a post graduate student at IIT Bombay, pursuing my Master's in the Integrated Circuits and Systems program (starting 2026). My interests lie in VLSI and digital IC design, chip architecture, FPGA and ASIC development, embedded systems, and PCB design.

I completed my B.Tech in Electrical Engineering from NIT Surat in 2023. After graduating, I worked for two years as an Automation Engineer at Vedanta, where I gained hands-on industry experience before deciding to pursue research and prepare for GATE, which brought me to IIT Bombay.
